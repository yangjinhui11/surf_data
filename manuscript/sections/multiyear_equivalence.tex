\subsection{Decadal autonomous integration}
\label{sec:multiyear}

The one-year autonomous comparison is extended to ten continuous CMFD years
(2009--2018, 366-day years included), each year restarted from the terminal
state of the previous year with the forcing linked continuously across the
year boundaries. No error growth with lead time occurs. At every annual
terminal state, soil-temperature relative RMSE stays below
$8\times10^{-4}$~\% across the decade, soil-water relative RMSE below
$2.5\times10^{-2}$~\%, and snow-water relative RMSE below
$3\times10^{-3}$~\%, the same magnitude as the one-year values of
Table~\ref{tab:annual-autonomous}. The oscillation is the signature of a
forced dissipative system with threshold-branch amplification: a
round-off difference can flip a discrete branch decision and produce a finite
jump, which soil diffusion and the surface-energy feedback then damp,
rather than chaotic error growth, which is absent by construction because
the atmospheric forcing is prescribed and identical in the two
implementations; each field's error peaks in its own threshold-active
season, and the terminal-error level is a quasi-stationary balance between
weather-set branch injection and diffusive damping, orders of magnitude
below the seasonal variability of the fields themselves. The
terminal-state comparison table, the annual error evolution, and the forcing
alignment that motivates the exact restart procedure
are collected in Appendix~\ref{app:multiyear}.
